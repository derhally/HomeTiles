Assist Sphere Options

Eleven looks for the Assist voice screen. Each preview is a 1024 × 600 panel, scaled down, with a simulated voice driving it. Options A to D are built only from circles, pills and arcs. Options E to I add lines, polylines and short number labels, still without per-pixel effects, so each can be drawn by LVGL on the ESP32-P4. Options J and K are drawn from Assist screen files, the JSON the panel loads.

Option A

Atom

Cyan, violet and magenta lobes swell with your voice. While thinking they gather into a small sphere and nine satellites circle it on three tilted orbits inside two counter-turning rings.

Feel
Colorful, scientific

Option B

Reactor

A bright core sends out sonar rings, faster and wider the louder you talk. While thinking, three broken rings spin at different speeds and directions like a HUD targeting reticle.

Feel
Sci-fi interface, cool blue
Objects
9 circles, 7 arcs
Option C

Dot Halo

Thirty-two dots ring a soft core. Your voice pushes each dot outward by a different amount, so the ring becomes a living waveform. While thinking, a bright comet chases around the ring.

Feel
Minimal, precise
Objects
35 circles
Option D

Voice Pills

Four colored dots stretch into pills that dance with each syllable. While thinking they circle each other, and while answering they bounce in a wave with the reply.

Feel
Friendly, familiar
Objects
4 pills, 1 glow
Option E

Sentinel

A heads-up display. Seventy-two ticks around the core rise with the voice like a round spectrum, inside broken rings and a turning triangle. While thinking the rings spin up, an amber arc races around and a scan line sweeps. While answering the ticks pulse in waves, with number readouts on both sides.

Feel
Sci-fi HUD, cyan and amber
Objects
~110 lines, 12 arcs, 3 circles, 5 short number labels
Panel
The soft glow is a wider faint line under each bright one.
Option F

Phosphor

A green oscilloscope on a terminal. The voice draws the trace, with fading afterglow copies behind it. While thinking the trace goes flat with a travelling blip and number rows print in the corner. While answering the trace curls into a Lissajous figure that grows with the reply. The caption is a typed prompt with a block cursor.

Feel
1980s terminal, green phosphor
Objects
4 polylines of 128 points, ~60 grid dots, 6 labels
Panel
Afterglow is three older traces at lower opacity. No CRT scanlines or bloom, which would need per-pixel work.
Option G

Light Disc

A glowing disc on a dark grid. Eight segments of its ring light with the voice. While thinking the disc turns and two light trails, cyan and orange, race the grid in right angles behind it. While answering the disc rings pulse with the reply.

Feel
Neon grid world, cyan and orange
Objects
25 grid lines, 12 arcs, 2 trails of up to 12 points
Panel
The grid is static and can be drawn once. Trails are two polylines.
Option H

Lens

A calm red lens in a brushed-metal ring. The glow around the bright centre grows with the voice. While thinking it breathes slowly. While answering it brightens with each word. Nothing else moves.

Feel
Still, watchful, red and steel
Objects
10 filled circles, 4 rings, 2 highlight arcs
Panel
The soft red falloff is stacked circles at rising opacity instead of a gradient. The cheapest option here.
Option I

Iris

A close-up eye. The pupil opens with the voice. While thinking an inspection box hunts across the iris with crosshairs, a scan line and coordinates. While answering the pupil and fibers pulse with the reply.

Feel
Noir, teal and amber
Objects
90 fiber lines, 4 circles, 8 bracket lines, 2 labels
Panel
The fibers barely change and can be cached as one image, rotated only while thinking.

Robot

A small robot in outline. Listening, it bobs, blinks, its ears glow and sound waves reach its head with your voice. Thinking, its eyes wander, the antenna blinks amber, a hand goes to its chin and three dots bounce above it. Answering, its mouth moves with the reply, its eyes turn happy and it waves.

Feel
Cute, friendly
Objects

Panel

Option K · screen file

Retro Robot

A tall tin-can head with a dome, an antenna, a wide visor and a grille mouth. Its eyes glance about while listening, its lids droop and a question mark bobs while thinking, and the grille teeth part with each word while answering.

Feel
Retro, cheeky
Objects
34 shapes at most
Panel
Drawn here from its JSON screen file, retro_robot.json.
Auto cycle runs 5 s listening, 3 s thinking and 4 s answering. On the panel the listening level comes from the microphone and the answering level from the speaker; here both are simulated.
